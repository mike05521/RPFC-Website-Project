About the club

Ownership perks. None of the ownership headaches.

Riverside Pilots Flying Club is a true club — owned and run by its members, not a rental counter. For four decades we've pooled our resources to keep a small, well-cared-for fleet ready to fly, so every member gets airplane-owner access at a fraction of the cost.

Aircraft are rented "wet" — fuel is included, so you fill up at any FBO and get reimbursed. Each aircraft has an assigned Plane Captain, a Maintenance Officer oversees mechanical status, and our Finance Committee reviews rates twice a year in meetings open to every member. It's transparent, it's affordable, and it's built by pilots who actually fly.

How to join

Four steps to your first flight.

We keep it personal on purpose — you'll meet the people you'll be flying and wrenching alongside before you ever sign.

Apply

Submit a membership application with a short introduction about yourself and your flying goals.

Meet the board

Attend a monthly meeting at Riverside Municipal to talk through your goals and get your questions answered.

Check out

Complete a checkout in each aircraft with a club instructor so you're safe, current, and confident.

Go flying

Book online, grab the keys, and fly — no daily minimums and no advance-notice hoops.
